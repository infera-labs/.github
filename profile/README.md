## About

Infera is a research and development team working across **artificial intelligence and software engineering**.

Founded by **Lorenzo Baiardi** and **Marco Bessi**, both with a master's degree in Artificial Intelligence, we bring a research mindset to the design and development of practical technology.

## Our focus

| Area | What we work on |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Artificial Intelligence** | Deep learning, intelligent applications, and automation. |
| **Computer Vision** | Image and video analysis, visual recognition, and perception systems. |
| **Software Engineering** | Software architecture, distributed systems, APIs, and full-stack applications. |
| **Drone Systems** | Aerial vision, telemetry, and software–hardware integration. |

## How we work

We study the problem, test different approaches, and develop solutions with reliability, maintainability, and long-term evolution in mind. Research and hands-on experimentation guide our work, from early prototypes to complete applications.

Our repositories bring together software projects, tools, and research experiments—each reflecting a different stage of exploration and development.
