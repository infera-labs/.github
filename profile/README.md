## About

Infera is a research and development team working across artificial intelligence and software engineering. We explore new technologies and turn promising ideas into practical software.

Founded by **Lorenzo Baiardi** and **Marco Bessi**, both of whom hold a master's degree in Artificial Intelligence, Infera brings together research, architectural design, and hands-on development.

## Areas of work

| Field | Focus |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Artificial Intelligence** | Deep learning, intelligent applications, and automation. |
| **Computer Vision** | Image and video analysis, visual recognition, and perception systems. |
| **Software Engineering** | Software architecture, scalable systems, APIs, and full-stack development. |

We take ideas from research and experimentation through to reliable, maintainable applications. Our repositories collect the projects, tools, and explorations behind that work.
